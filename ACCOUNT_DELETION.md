# Delete your KidSizes account

**App:** KidSizes — children's clothing sizes, growth and measurements tracker
**Developer:** Oleg Bogumirski · obogumirski@gmail.com

You can delete your KidSizes account and the data linked to it at any time.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## In the app (Android and iOS)

1. Open KidSizes and sign in.
2. Open **Settings** and scroll to the **Account** section.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. If your account has a password, enter it, then tap **Delete permanently**.

## On the web

1. Go to [kidsizes.olegb.dev](https://kidsizes.olegb.dev) and sign in.
2. Open **Settings** → **Delete account** and confirm.

## By e-mail

If you can't sign in, write to **obogumirski@gmail.com** from the e-mail
address of your account with the subject "Delete my KidSizes account".
We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

Right away and permanently:

- your account: e-mail address, name, password hash, Google or Apple sign-in
  link, settings, push notification tokens;
- every child profile you own, with its measurements, clothing sizes, teeth
  records, vocabulary, allergies, photos (with their thumbnails), change
  history, sharing access and invitations;
- your backup archives and label scans.

Child profiles that other people shared with you stay with their owners:
only your access is removed, and your past edits there remain without your name.

## What is kept and for how long

- **Database backups** at our hosting provider — up to 14 days, after which
  they are overwritten.
- **Crash reports** — technical error details without your name, e-mail or
  photos — up to 90 days.
