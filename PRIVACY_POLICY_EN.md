# Privacy Policy — KidSizes

Effective date: July 26, 2026

“KidSizes” helps parents track their children’s clothing and shoe sizes,
height, weight and other measurements. We respect your privacy and collect
only the data required for the app to work. We do not use data for tracking,
we do not show ads, and we do not sell data to third parties.

## Data we collect

Data is stored on our server and linked to your account:

- **Email address** — to create an account, sign in, and recover access.
- **User content about children** that you enter yourself: name, date of
  birth, sex, measurements (height, weight, foot size, body measurements),
  and photos if you add them.
- **Device identifier (push token)** — only if you enable push
  notifications; used to deliver reminders.

We do **not** collect location data, do not use third-party advertising or
analytics SDKs, and do not track you across apps and websites.

## How we use data

- Running the app: authentication, storing and displaying the measurements
  and child profiles you enter.
- Sending reminders (push and/or email) if you enabled them.

## Sign in with Apple

If you use “Sign in with Apple”, we receive only the account identifier and,
with your consent, an email address to create your profile.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Data may be
processed by our hosting/cloud provider solely to operate the service.

## Retention and deletion

Data is stored while your account exists. You can delete your account and
associated data in the app settings or by request to the contact email
below. After deletion, data is removed from the server.

## Children

The app is intended for parents and guardians. Data about children is entered
by an adult account holder. The app is not intended for independent use by
children and does not collect data directly from children.

## Security

We use reasonable technical measures (HTTPS transport encryption, hashed
password storage) to protect your data.
